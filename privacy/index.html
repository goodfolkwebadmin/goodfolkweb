Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

Goodfolk builds and hosts websites for local businesses. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit goodfolkweb.com, request a free preview, or become a client, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you fill out a form on our site or on Facebook or Instagram, we collect what you enter, such as your name, phone number, email, business name, type of business, town, and any links you share.
  • Messages. If you email us or use the chat on our site, we keep those messages so we can reply.
  • Payment details. Payments are handled by Stripe. We see your name, email, and the last four digits of your card, but we never see or store your full card number.
  • Website usage. We use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand how people find and use our site, such as pages visited and general location. These tools use cookies.

How we use it

  • To build your free preview website and contact you about it
  • To reply to your questions and provide the services you sign up for
  • To send occasional updates or offers by email, which you can unsubscribe from at any time
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the services that help us run Goodfolk, and only as needed:

  • Stripe, for payments
  • Google (Analytics and Workspace email) and Meta (Facebook and Instagram ads and lead forms)
  • FormSubmit, which delivers our website form messages to our inbox
  • MailerLite, for our email list

We may also share information if required by law.

Your choices

  • Ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you by emailing hello@goodfolkweb.com.
  • Unsubscribe from our emails using the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it to work with you, and for client records as required for taxes and accounting. If you ask us to delete it, we will, unless we are required to keep it.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email hello@goodfolkweb.com.
