Client Agreement

Last updated October 23, 2026

This agreement is between Goodfolk ("we," "us") and the business that buys a website from us ("you"). When you check the box to accept these terms and pay at checkout, you agree to everything on this page. Please read it. It's written in plain English on purpose.

The short version

  • You pay a one-time build fee up front. Right now that's $499.
  • Your site comes with the Care Plan, $29 a month, for hosting and edits. It starts 30 days after you pay.
  • If a payment goes unpaid for 14 days, your site is taken offline until it's paid.
  • You can cancel anytime. When you do, your site comes down and we transfer your domain to you.
  • No hidden fees.

1. What you're buying

You're buying two things: a website we design and build for your business (the "Build"), and ongoing hosting and care for that website (the "Care Plan"). The Care Plan is required for as long as your website is live with us.

2. Price and payment

  • Build fee. The build fee is the one-time price shown at checkout, paid in full before we build your live site. During our Harvest Special, available through October 31, 2026, the build fee is $499. Our regular build fee is $950.
  • Care Plan. $29 a month. Your first month starts 30 days after you pay the build fee, and then it renews automatically every month on the card you used at checkout.
  • Domains. A standard domain (such as a .com) is included in the Care Plan. If you want a premium or unusual domain that costs more than $25 a year, we'll tell you the cost first and bill it to you at cost.
  • Not included. Email at your domain (for example, you@yourbusiness.com) isn't included. We can set it up for you at cost or help you set it up in your own account.
  • Price changes. If we ever change the Care Plan price, we'll email you at least 30 days before it takes effect.

3. How the build works

  • You see it first. You approve a preview of your website before you pay.
  • You send us your materials. After you pay, we'll ask for things like your logo, photos, hours, and domain login if you already have one.
  • We launch fast. We aim to have your site live within 7 days of receiving your materials. If we're waiting on something from you, the clock waits too.
  • Two rounds of revisions. Before launch, you get up to two rounds of changes, such as text, colors, and photos. New pages or a different design are extra, and we'll quote them before doing any work.

4. What the Care Plan includes

  • Hosting, security (SSL), and keeping your site online
  • Registering and renewing your standard domain
  • Your contact form, delivered to your email
  • Up to 2 edits a month, such as updating hours, prices, photos, or a service. Send edit requests by email to hello@goodfolkweb.com or through our edit request form. We complete them within 2 business days. Unused edits don't carry over to the next month.

Larger work, like new pages, a redesign, online booking, or an online store, isn't part of the Care Plan. We'll quote it separately, and you can say no.

5. If a payment doesn't go through

If a Care Plan payment fails, we'll let you know by email, and our payment system will try your card again. If the payment is still unpaid 14 days after its due date, we'll take your website offline. As soon as you pay what's owed, we'll put it back up. If it's still unpaid after 60 days, we'll treat it as a cancellation (see section 6).

6. Cancelling

  • You can cancel anytime, with no cancellation fee. Cancel from your billing link or by emailing hello@goodfolkweb.com.
  • Your site stays up through the month you've paid for. We don't refund partial months.
  • Then your site comes down. At the end of your last paid month, we take your website and contact form offline.
  • Your domain goes back to you. We'll transfer your domain to an account in your name and walk you through it. If you'd like your site files, just ask and we'll send them to you.

7. Refunds

Because you approve your preview before you pay, the build fee isn't refundable once we've started building your live site. If we can't deliver your website at all, we'll refund your build fee in full.

8. Who owns what

  • Yours: once your build fee is paid, you own your business content (your words, photos, logo, and information), your domain, and the finished website we built for you.
  • Ours: we keep our own accounts, tools, and logins, and the general templates and code we use to build websites for all of our clients. We may keep using them for other clients. We won't reuse your content or branding.

9. Your content

You're responsible for the content you give us. By sending it, you confirm that you have the right to use it, including photos, logos, and reviews, and that your business information, such as prices, hours, and licenses, is accurate. Let us know when something changes, and we'll update it as one of your monthly edits.

10. Credit and examples

We may include a small "Website by Goodfolk" link in your site's footer, and we may show your website as an example of our work. If you'd rather we didn't do either, tell us and we'll remove it.

11. Limits

We work hard to keep your website online and working, but we rely on outside services for hosting, domains, and forms, and we can't promise your site will never go down. If something goes wrong, we'll fix it as quickly as we can. We aren't responsible for lost business or other indirect losses, and our total responsibility under this agreement is limited to the amount you've paid us in the 3 months before the issue.
